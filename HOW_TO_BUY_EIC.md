# HOW TO BUY & TRADE EIC

*Energy Intelligence Coin (EIC) - Base Mainnet*

## Overview

This document explains how to acquire and trade **Energy Intelligence Coin
(EIC)** on the **Base Mainnet** network.

EIC is the token identity. The asset framework is maintained by **Energy
Intelligence Coin Emergent Technologies**, while public documentation and
stewardship materials are presented under **EIC Foundations**.

There is **no centralized sale portal** at this time. Trading is done entirely
through decentralized markets.

## 1. Wallet Requirements

To buy or trade EIC, you must use a crypto wallet that supports the **Base**
network.

Examples:
- MetaMask
- Coinbase Wallet
- Rabby Wallet

If you are bridging from Ethereum Mainnet, bridge your ETH into **Base** before
swapping. Your wallet must be connected to the Base network in order to
transact.

## 2. Verify the Official EIC Contract

Before you swap any tokens, verify the **official EIC contract address**:

1. Use the contract address published in this repository.
2. Cross-check on BaseScan.
3. Confirm token name, symbol, and decimals match what is documented here.

Canonical contract:
- `0x867776d88DfD7061324FD97C8e03fb2DcC29a024`

Never trust a contract address copied from social media comments.

## 3. Method A - Swap for EIC

To acquire EIC:

1. Go to the Aerodrome DEX interface on Base.
2. Connect your wallet.
3. Set the swap parameters:
   - **From:** WETH
   - **To:** EIC
4. Adjust slippage tolerance as needed.
5. Confirm and submit the transaction.

After the transaction completes, you should see your EIC balance in your
wallet.

## 4. Method B - Provide Liquidity (Advanced)

If you want to participate more deeply:

1. Supply **equal values** of WETH and EIC into the liquidity pool.
2. Deposit them into the **WETH/EIC pool**.
3. Receive liquidity provider (LP) tokens.

Important: **LP tokens represent ownership of pooled assets. They are not EIC
itself.**

## 5. Market Structure

- Initial liquidity may be managed deliberately to reduce manipulation risk.
- There is currently **no public mint function** available.
- All EIC acquisition is market-based via swaps or liquidity participation.
- There is **no centralized treasury sale** described in this repository.

## 6. Risk Disclosure

EIC is:
- **Early stage**
- **Experimental**
- **Volatile**

Do not trade funds you cannot afford to lose. Always verify contract
authenticity and trading parameters before interacting.
