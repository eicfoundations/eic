# EIC Paper (Draft)

## Abstract
Energy Intelligence Coin (EIC) is a live Base Mainnet asset with a
documentation-first public posture in Observed Mode. This document defines
scope, posture, and constraints without activating wallet flows, commerce,
claims, or reward mechanics on the public site.

## Identity Structure
- Digital Asset: Energy Intelligence Coin (EIC)
- Organization: Energy Intelligence Coin Emergent Technologies
- Community / Stewardship Arm: EIC Foundations

## Definitions
- EIC: Energy Intelligence Coin, the Base Mainnet digital asset documented here.
- Observed Mode: The current state where information is view-only and inactive
  modules are clearly labeled.
- EIC Foundations: The public community, documentation, governance, and
  stewardship identity.
- Energy Intelligence Coin Emergent Technologies: The maintaining organization
  for the asset framework and technical direction.
- POI: Proof of Insight, a conceptual validation of understanding. POI is not
  active, provides no rewards, and confers no access or status in Draft /
  Observed Mode.

## Status & Disclosure
EIC is in Draft / Observed Mode. Public information is descriptive, not
promotional. POI and Store remain inactive and labeled as such. Certain
operational details may be disclosed later for security-first reasons.

## Mission & Principles
EIC centers human agency and voluntary engagement. Voluntary participation is
paramount, and non-manipulation is required. Security-first posture and neutral
language guide all public-facing material.

## System Overview
High-level components include:
- Public website and documentation under EIC Foundations
- Stewardship and governance context in Observed Mode
- Token layer: Energy Intelligence Coin (live on Base Mainnet)
- Validation layer (POI: conceptual and inactive)

## Safety & Ethics (Binding)
The Safety & Ethics Checklist at `/ethics` is binding for all EIC systems and
future modules. If a feature conflicts with the checklist, the feature does not
ship.

## Threat Model & Security Posture
EIC assumes phishing and impersonation risks and maintains an official link
policy. EIC is non-custodial and never requests secret material. Releases are
phased and validated before public exposure.

## Participation Model (Conceptual)

Steward, and Maintainer where applicable. Participants can disengage at any time
without penalty. No onboarding flows are active in Draft / Observed Mode.
